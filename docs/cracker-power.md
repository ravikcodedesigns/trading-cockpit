# Cracker Power Table — minimum detectable effect (MDE)

> Generated by `cracker_p23_power.ts` · data through **2026-07-06** · re-publish as forward days accrue.
> MDE = 2.8 × SE(day-block bootstrap, B=2000) of the uniq_w-weighted mean drift-adjusted markout —
> the smallest TRUE effect (in points) detectable at 80% power / α=0.05 with n visits.
> **Usage rule (frozen):** a Phase-3 factor is testable at its available n only if its plausible
> effect ≥ MDE(n). Below that → pre-declared UNTESTABLE, queued for forward accumulation.
> Effects screened on the MICRO store must first be shrunk by the P0.4 attenuation
> (×0.8 NQ, ×0.65 ES) before comparison — equivalently use the "micro-screen MDE" columns.

### Reading it

- Row = markout horizon; column = visits available to the factor bucket being tested.
- Example: a factor bucket with 200 visits can only prove effects ≥ the @n=200 cell; a
  plausible-but-smaller effect must WAIT for more days rather than be declared null.
- The "micro-screen" column is the bar for effects measured on micro data but claimed
  for the mini market (P0.4 attenuation ×0.8 NQ / ×0.65 ES).
- Companion baselines (same data, cracker_p21_selftest.ts): placebo hold-rate band and the
  visit-mechanics continuation curve — Phase-3 comparisons are VISITS-vs-VISITS against these,
  never vs random-time or analytic nulls.
